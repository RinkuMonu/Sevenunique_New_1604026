# Remaining Payment Solution Images

Add all files to this folder: `public/images/payment-solutions/services/`.

## Common visual reference

- Landscape 3:2 website photo, minimum 1536 x 1024.
- Photorealistic Indian people and an unmistakably Indian environment.
- Natural daylight, professional, trustworthy, uncluttered composition.
- No US dollars, foreign currency, visible cash, logos, watermark, collage, or readable personal data.
- Use generic screens/documents; avoid distorted hands and AI-generated text.
